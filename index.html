About Queer Ass Folk

More than a gig night: a space for connection, celebration and queer joy.

Born in Hastings and now reaching new heights, we centre LGBTQ+ voices, champion accessibility, and build community through live music.

Come as you are, stay as long as you like, and join us in redefining what a music night can be. We’re here, we’re loud (sometimes), and we’re building something beautiful together.

Follow along on Instagram.

Our team

  • Hayz Harland

    they/them · Co-founder

    Hayz Harland, AKA Tygermylk, is a force to be reckoned with. Their debut album Local Girl, Always Tired, ten years in the making, is a powerful coming-of-age record shaped by grief, resilience, chronic illness and tireless advocacy for LGBTQIA+ rights and disability inclusion.

    An established live performer, Tygermylk cut their teeth busking on London’s Southbank. Performances at Glastonbury, Isle of Wight Festival and Channel 4’s The Last Leg have cemented their reputation on stage. In 2024, they co-founded Queer Ass Folk to create the kind of intentional, community-led live spaces they longed for as an emerging queer artist.

  • Charlie Austen

    she/her · Co-founder

    Charlie Austen is a one-woman tour-de-force across folk, soul and rock. With airy yet powerful, blues-infused vocals, intricate guitar work and her signature barefoot suitcase drum-kit, she weaves together stories of life, death, joy, heartbreak and queer history.

    An extensive UK tourer with dates in Europe and beyond, and features on national radio, Charlie brings industry experience and grassroots spirit. In 2024, she co-founded Queer Ass Folk to carve out intentional, affirming live spaces for LGBTQ+ artists and audiences alike.
